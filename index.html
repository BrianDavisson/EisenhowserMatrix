📋 How to Use the Eisenhower Matrix

The Four Quadrants

Q1 — DO (Urgent + Important)

Handle immediately — today. Crises, deadlines, urgent problems. Use the Eat the Frog principle and tackle these first thing.

Q2 — DECIDE (Important, Not Urgent)

Schedule these for a specific date. Long-term goals, planning, development. High performers spend most of their time here.

Q3 — DELEGATE (Urgent, Not Important)

Assign to someone else. Certain meetings, routine requests, interruptions. Always track delegated items to ensure completion.

Q4 — DELETE (Not Urgent, Not Important)

Eliminate entirely. Time wasters, mindless browsing, irrelevant tasks. Log them as "not-to-dos" to build awareness.

Using This App

  • ⌨️Type a task in any quadrant and press Enter or + to add it
  • ✅Click the checkbox to mark a task complete (strikethrough)
  • ↗️Hover a task and click the arrow icon to move it to another quadrant
  • 🖱️On desktop, drag and drop tasks between quadrants
  • ✏️Double-click any task text to edit it inline
  • ×Click × to permanently delete a task
  • 💾All tasks are automatically saved to this device
  • 📱Add to your phone's home screen for an app-like experience

Pro Tips

  • 🐸Eat the frog first — start each day with your hardest Q1 task for momentum
  • 🎯Keep a maximum of 8 tasks per quadrant to avoid overwhelm
  • 📵Aim to spend most of your time in Q2 — it prevents Q1 crises from forming
  • ⏱️Use the Pomodoro technique (25 min focused sessions) for Q1 tasks
  • 🌅Plan the evening before, review and adjust each morning
  • 🔁Reassess your quadrants weekly — priorities shift over time
